The Last 40 Million — Financial Exclusion in Nigeria

📊 26,930 Respondents · 6 Modules · Live Dashboard · 14-Page Report

Inferential statistical analysis of EFInA's 2023 nationally representative survey to identify which excluded Nigerians are financially ready but structurally blocked — and what each group needs. Six analytical modules covering demographic profiling, barrier analysis, chi-square hypothesis testing, logistic regression on 26,361 observations (McFadden R² = 0.2590), and a natural experiment on the Naira redesign policy shock. Key finding: education predicts exclusion at 34x odds ratio, and financial exclusion in Nigeria is primarily a poverty problem, not a distance problem. Only 0.8% of excluded adults responded to the 2023 cash crunch.
